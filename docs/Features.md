is a design an implementer can pick up without a conversation first.

hard-won context moves to [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) and its entry here is
deleted.

Conventions in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) apply to everything below,
including the module contract and the dry-run guarantee.
